Why we exist

Most entrepreneurship education starts in college. We think it should start earlier.

Entrepreneurship education

Real frameworks for spotting a problem, testing an idea, and building something around it — scaled to a middle or high schooler, not a business school.

Financial literacy

Budgeting, saving, and the basic economics of running something — the parts of money most students never see in a standard classroom.

Youth-led, by design

Every camp and workshop is built and taught by teens close in age to the students in the room — not adults guessing what teenagers want to hear.

Genesis started with a simple observation from its founders: the entrepreneurship experiences that changed how they thought about problem-solving weren't available to most students their age — so they built the version that is.
