Our Approach

“Guiding Emerging Next-Gen Entrepreneurial Success through inclusive programs designed by youth, for youth.”

Built around one idea: students learn best from people close to their own age.

01

Peer-taught, from the ground up

Every camp and workshop at Genesis is designed and led by teens, not adults guessing what teenagers want to hear.

02

Entrepreneurship and money, together

Genesis pairs hands-on entrepreneurship with financial literacy: budgeting, saving, and the basics of running something, in the same program.

03

Started by a national finalist

Co-founder Siddharth Kommuri placed 8th nationally and 3rd regionally out of 650+ teams in a virtual entrepreneurship simulation. Genesis is the program he built so more students could have that shot sooner.
