Get Involved

Three ways to be part of Genesis.

However you'd like to get involved, the fastest way to reach the team directly is Instagram. That's where camp dates, applications, and updates go out first.

01

Enroll a student

Sign up for the High School Head Start Camp or a workshop, open to middle and early high school students in the Austin area. No prior experience with business or entrepreneurship is expected going in.

Message us

02

Partner with us

Schools, youth organizations, and local businesses can partner on camps, space, or mentorship. If you're not sure whether what you have in mind fits, ask anyway.

Email the team

03

Follow along

Camp dates, application windows, and student projects are posted first on Instagram, usually before anywhere else, including this site.

@thegenesisinitiative_austin

What Happens Next

Reaching out is the whole first step.

Send a message

Instagram DM for students and families, email for schools and partners. Say roughly what you're looking for, enrolling a student, partnering, or just following along.

Hear back from an actual founder

Because the team is small and youth-led, there's no call center in between. A response comes from Hasini or Siddharth directly, not a generic account.

Get the specifics that fit your situation

Exact dates, cost, and logistics vary by camp and are confirmed at this point, since they're the kind of details that change season to season and shouldn't be guessed at in advance.

Pricing depends on the specific camp or workshop and is shared when you reach out, rather than fixed here, since it can vary by season and format.
Current programming is in-person in the Greater Austin area. If you're outside that area and still interested, it's worth reaching out directly, since that's exactly the kind of demand that shapes where Genesis expands next.
Genesis is youth-led, but it isn't youth-only when it comes to support. Space, mentorship, and local business partnerships all go through the same "partner with us" channel above.
