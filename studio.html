The Program

  • Stage — 01

    Find something worth solving

    Sessions start by looking outward, not at spreadsheets: noticing a real, specific problem, rather than starting from an idea in search of one.

    Looking outwardValidation
  • Stage — 02

    Build it cheap, test it fast

    A rough answer, in front of people, quickly — alongside the money side: what it would cost, what it might charge, whether the math survives contact with reality.

    PrototypingBudgeting
  • Stage — 03

    Pitch it to the room

    Every camp ends with students presenting what they built to the group and to teen mentors, out loud — its own separate skill from having a good idea.

    PresentingFeedback
  • Camp — Austin

    High School Head Start Camp

    The in-person camp where all of it happens, for middle and early high school students in the Greater Austin area. No prior experience expected.
