Who's Behind It

Two students built this. They're still the ones running it.

There's no adult board signing off on the curriculum here. Genesis is run day-to-day by two students who each arrived at the same conclusion from different directions, entrepreneurship education is more useful the earlier it happens, and decided the fastest way to fix that was to build the program themselves rather than wait for someone older to get around to it.

HC

Co-Founder & CEO

Hasini Chandrakumar

Based in Leander, TX, Hasini co-founded The Genesis Initiative to make entrepreneurship and financial literacy education accessible to students who wouldn't otherwise encounter it before college. She's competed at ICDC, DECA's international conference.

SK

Co-Founder

Siddharth Kommuri

A student at Rouse High School, Siddharth placed 8th nationally and 3rd regionally out of 650+ teams in a virtual entrepreneurship simulation. That experience convinced him more students deserved a shot at it.

LinkedIn

Neither founder set out to run a nonprofit. Hasini wanted entrepreneurship and financial literacy to reach students before college closed that gap by default. Siddharth wanted more students to get the kind of experience a national competition gave him, without having to stumble into it by luck. Genesis is what happened when those two goals turned out to be the same goal.

Growing The Team

Right now, it's two people. That's expected to change.

As Genesis takes on more camps and more students, the plan is to bring on more teen instructors and mentors alongside the founders, not to keep everything running through two people indefinitely. If that's something you'd want to be part of, that starts with the same first step as everything else here.
