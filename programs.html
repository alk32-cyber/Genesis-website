Program

High School Head Start Camp

Grades 6–9

Get a head start on the skills school doesn't teach yet.

A hands-on introduction to entrepreneurship and money fundamentals, built and led by teen instructors so the pace, examples, and language actually meet students where they are.

FormatIn-person camp & workshops
AudienceMiddle & early high school
LocationGreater Austin area
  • Finding and validating a real problem worth solving
  • Budgeting and the basics of managing money
  • Building and pitching an idea as a team
  • Mentorship from teen founders, not just adults

Upcoming dates and registration are posted first on @thegenesisinitiative_austin.
