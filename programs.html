01 / Program

High School Head Start Camp

Get a head start on the skills school doesn't teach yet.

A hands-on introduction to entrepreneurship and money fundamentals, built and led by teen instructors so the pace, examples, and language actually meet students where they are.

FormatIn-person camp & workshops
AudienceMiddle & early high school
LocationGreater Austin area
  • Finding and validating a real problem worth solving
  • Budgeting and the basics of managing money
  • Building and pitching an idea as a team
  • Mentorship from teen founders, not just adults

02 / Method

From a real problem to a real pitch.

Every session moves in the same direction. The stage on the left holds while the three stages pass it.

Stage 01 / Find the problem
Stage 02 / Build it cheap, test it fast
Stage 03 / Pitch it to the room

Stage 01

Find something worth solving

Early sessions are spent looking outward before looking at spreadsheets: noticing a real, specific problem worth paying attention to, rather than starting from a business idea in search of a problem.

Stage 02

Build it cheap, test it fast

Once a problem is real, the group sketches a rough answer to it and gets it in front of people quickly, alongside the money side: what it would cost, what it might charge, whether the math survives contact with reality.

Stage 03

Pitch it to the room

Every camp ends with students presenting what they built to the group and to teen mentors, out loud, in front of people, which is its own separate skill from having a good idea in the first place.
